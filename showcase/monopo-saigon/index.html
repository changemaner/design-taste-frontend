THE SILENCE

Three quiet rules

RULE 01

No chrome

The card is content, not a container. No frames, no fills, no chrome.

RULE 02

One red

One iridescent moment per page, always in the light, never on a control.

RULE 03

Wide gaps

Spacious 46–128px breathing room. The gallery is meant to be walked.
