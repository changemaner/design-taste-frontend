PANIFICIO NAPOLI
THE MENU

Daily chalkboard

FIRST SERVICE

Bread rolls, 6 AM — the workhorse of the block. Focaccia at 9, still warm.

SECOND SERVICE

Sfogliatella at noon, taralli at four. The yellow board sells out by five.

THIRD SERVICE

Panettone by appointment. Christmas is a color band, not a season.

THE BLUE HOUR

Taralli with olives, one hour before close. The blue accent never oversells.
