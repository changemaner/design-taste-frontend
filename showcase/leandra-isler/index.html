PRAXIS LEANDRA ISLER HERBARIUM
THE PROCESS

Four quiet steps

01 Gather at dawn Cut before the dew dries; the shape is truest then.
02 Press six weeks Between blotters and boards. No heat, no hurry.
03 Mount on vellum A sheet of sand, a ruled caption, a single needle of glue.
04 File by season The herbarium is ordered by month, not by name.
